- The first published description of double-entry bookkeeping was in Luca Pacioli's mathematics textbook, printed in Venice in 1494. [Double-entry bookkeeping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double-entry_bookkeeping)
- The oldest surviving full double-entry ledger dates from 1299 to 1300, kept by Amatino Manucci, a Florentine merchant working for a firm in Nîmes. [Double-entry bookkeeping](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Double-entry_bookkeeping)
